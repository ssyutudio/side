Pro is not on sale yet. The price goes to the waitlist first, and nothing is charged before we write to you. When it opens, Lemon Squeezy handles payment, tax and invoices, and your license key goes in Side → Settings → Side Pro.

What Side Pro sends

Tab titles with temporary ids, your one line for tomorrow, your step counts (numbers only), file names without folders from the desktop apps you turn on, and the line you type in Ask sidy. They go through the Side server to Claude (Anthropic). The server checks your license and counts requests; it keeps none of the text. Addresses, page text, file contents and excluded sites never leave your computer. You can turn AI drafting off in Settings at any time.

Fair use

Side Pro includes up to 200 Claude requests a day per license. A heavy day of real use is a few dozen. Past the limit, Side uses simple rules until the next day.
