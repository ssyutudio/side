sidy — one character, four states, every size

A rounded square, one ink line, a blue side that leans on the screen edge, two pill eyes looking left at your work. No mouth, cheeks, sparkles, gradients or oversized eyes. Drawn in SVG; at the edge sidy is Small (40 px) by default.

Sizes — same drawing from Dock icon to Large

Rules the build keeps

  1. Body #F3F3EF, ink #1E2025 (line ≈ 5% of the side), blue side #3B54E7 ≈ 26% of the width. Corner radius ≈ 28%.
  2. Eyes: two vertical pills at 20% and 42% of the width, a little above centre. They always look left, toward the work.
  3. Quiet = tucked in: eyes only, the blue side fully behind the edge (≈ 48% visible). Talking or hovered = leans out until the blue side shows (≈ 92%). 320 ms ease-out; none with Reduce Motion. No idle movement.
  4. Hidden = 0 px. No handle, dot or sliver. ⌃⌥S or the Dock brings it back.
  5. Reactions (unlock, video, music) play once for about 2 s, then sidy is still again. See the reactions board.
  6. Click area is at least 48 × 48 px around the visible body (dashed box), even at Small.
