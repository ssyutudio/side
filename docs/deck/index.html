The problem

Some nights you can't stop.
Most mornings you can't start.

They are the same problem. You keep going tonight because you're not sure the flow will come back tomorrow, and tomorrow the unfinished pile makes it harder to begin.

Every plan I didn't follow was the planner mocking me.

Calm Diligence (Substack), on planners and ADHD

Reviewing things every 10 mins destroys my attention span.

Hacker News comment on working with AI coding agents

Backlog shame came up in five separate places we read, and so did "TODO bankruptcy". These show that the problem exists, not how common it is.

The insight

You don't need a better plan.
You need a select 1.

Our founder's trick: when work won't start, open the tool and run select 1. One result appears on screen, and a few minutes later you are working. Side prepares that first step for you, and every step it offers passes four checks.

1No judgment

Nothing to decide. It only opens what you left.

2Can't fail

A real page that exists, never a site you excluded.

3In the real tool

Your doc, your repo. Not a to-do list about them.

4Instant result

You see it right away, at the paragraph where you stopped.

How it works

Four beats, one small character at the edge of your screen.

Set Aside

Night. You decide to stop.

Your real tabs fold into named Chrome groups with one confirm. One optional line: What are you most curious about tomorrow? Undo brings everything back.

We count: tabs set aside, lines left for tomorrow

Be Beside

Flow, rest, three days away.

sidy waits at the screen edge, eyes only. No nagging, no backlog. "One more hour?" is never blocked: you get one fact and one check-in at a time you choose.

We count: times sidy spoke (fewer is better)

Step Inside

The next day, you drift back.

One bubble, one step: the page you stopped on opens at the paragraph, marked "You stopped here". Nothing opens until you choose.

We count: steps clicked, seconds to work

By your side

Days and weeks.

Side keeps a record of which steps got you going and whether you kept at it ten minutes later. The next step learns from it.

We count: still working 10 min after a step

What Side won't do

Trust is the product.

Never

  • Finish your work for you
  • Shut things off or block you when time's up
  • Show backlogs or failure-streak counts
  • Encourage all-nighters
  • Promise what sleep will do for you
  • Counsel your mood or diagnose you
  • Compare you with other people
  • Let an employer watch employees
  • Capture your screen

Your data

  • Addresses and page text never leave your computer.
  • Tab titles go to Claude only to name your groups. One switch turns it off.
  • Sites you exclude are never read, saved or sent.
  • What Side saw lists every tab Side read, skipped and sent.

Not in this build

Login · uploading tab contents · conversational planning · calendar and mail integration · payment · learning your type · Windows and mobile · a Korean interface
